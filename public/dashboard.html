About SAARTHI

SAARTHI (meaning Charioteer or Guide) is an advanced, data-driven agricultural intelligence platform designed to empower Indian farmers with precision agriculture.

Precision Recommendation

Analyzes your soil, water source, acres, and climate data to suggest the most profitable and suitable crops.

Market Demand

Provides live market demand and price estimates in nearby mandis based on your location.

AI Chat Companion

An intelligent, multi-lingual chatbot to answer any crop questions, soil management queries, or pesticide advice.

Disease Diagnostics

Computer-vision powered diagnosis of plant diseases directly from leaf photos with instant treatment advice.

Developed with by the CSE Students of CMRCET to bridge the gap between AI technology and rural farmers.

Expert Farming Tips

Smart Irrigation

Irrigate early in the morning or late in the evening to minimize water evaporation and reduce root disease risks.

Organic Pest Control

Mix 5ml Neem Oil with a few drops of dish soap in 1 liter of water. Spray on leaf undersides to repel aphids and mites organically.

Soil Testing

Test your farm soil pH every two years. Balanced soil pH (6.0 - 7.0) maximizes crop nutrient absorption and crop yield potential.

Government Agritech Schemes

PM-KISAN Yojana

Direct income support of ₹6,000 per year in three equal installments to all landholding farmer families across the country.

PM Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)

Crop insurance protection against natural calamities, pests, and diseases at a minimal premium of 2% for Kharif and 1.5% for Rabi crops.

Micro Irrigation Fund (MIF)

Subsidized loans and financial assistance to install drip and sprinkler irrigation networks to conserve water resources.
