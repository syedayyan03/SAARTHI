Right Crop. Right Time. Right Guidance.

SAARTHI helps farmers choose the best crop for their land by using soil type, water source, climate, market demand, and previous years data – in simple local languages.

  • Multi‑language support: Telugu, Hindi, English, Marathi, Malayalam
  • Crop recommendation with budget estimation
  • Market demand near you
  • Chatbox guidance and disease detection

Farmer‑first Design

Clean, simple screens with big buttons, clear colors, and optional voice instructions so every farmer can use it comfortably.

  • Optional Voice Guidance
  • High-Contrast & Big Buttons
  • Offline-Ready Models
  • 100% Mobile Responsive

Main Features

Crop Recommendation

Suggests 4–5 best crops and estimated budget based on your soil, water source, acres, and local conditions.

Market Demand Near You

Shows demand and price trends of important crops in nearby markets.

Chatbox Guidance

Gemini‑like conversational assistant for quick farming questions and guidance (prototype version here).

Disease Detector

Upload crop or leaf photos and get likely disease and precaution suggestions (ML model integration planned).

Contact & Team Details

SAARTHI is a student-developed project by second-year's Computer Science students of CMRCET (CMR College of Engineering & Technology). Our goal is to leverage technology to support farmers with intelligent crop recommendations, market insights, and AI-powered assistance. This project is built as part of our academic learning initiative to explore real-world problem solving in agriculture using web development and data-driven solutions.
